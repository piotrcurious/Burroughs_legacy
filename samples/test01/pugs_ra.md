TWO PUGS IN BABYLON

A Dread Transmission concerning the departure of two souls, the occupation of two bodies, and the continuing business of being president.

Dubwise, from the terrestrial kennel. All systems nominal. Jah know.

Morning come grey over Babylon. Rain pon the window, electric kettle singing little dub, and two pug-dem sleeping like unpaid civil servants.

One white, one black.

Misha and Winston.

I-and-I owner know dem seven year. Know every snore, every fart, every little footstep pon the kitchen floor when dem hear plastic wrapper. Know the white one love him tennis ball, and the black one love him own reflection, although him never quite trust the fellow in the mirror.

Then come Tuesday.

Tuesday bring no apocalypse, no trumpet from heaven, no earthquake. Just two dogs waking up with somebody else business inside dem.

Misha, the white one, get up, walk to the kitchen, inspect the refrigerator, inspect the door, inspect the hallway. Then him sit down and stare at the television.

Winston, the black one, approach the food bowl.

Him eat Misha breakfast.

Then him eat him own.

Then him look at I-and-I like the distribution system have been improperly administered.

"What's going on, bredren?"

Winston bark.

Misha don't bark.

Misha look at the door.

A strange morning.

Babylon breakfast.

---

The first thing change was play.

Misha have always been a joyful little idiot. Ball come, Misha run. Ball go, Misha run. Ball disappear beneath furniture, Misha conduct archaeological investigation with nose and paws until the ball emerge covered in dust, dog hair and ancient domestic secrets.

Now him don't play.

I-and-I throw the ball.

Misha watch it.

The ball roll beneath the table.

Misha look at the table.

Then him turn around and go sleep.

I-and-I try rubber chicken.

Nothing.

Try squeaky bone.

Nothing.

Try chicken-shaped toy with little red hat.

Misha examine the object with the suspicion of a customs official discovering undeclared currency.

"Come on, man. Is a toy."

Misha turn away.

The black one develop more interesting problem.

Winston discover him black coat.

Every morning him inspect himself in the mirror. Him look left. Look right. Examine back. Examine front. Then bark.

Him try lie down pon white towel.

Him try occupy white dog's bed.

Him roll pon white laundry.

I-and-I buy special white blanket.

Winston immediately claim it.

Him lie down, stretch out, turn around, inspect him paws.

Still black.

Him bark again.

"Easy, bredren. Is only fur."

Winston stare at I-and-I.

There is something profoundly presidential about the expression.

---

The first evidence come through television.

News programme running about Moscow. Big motorcade, long corridor, men in dark suits, doors open and close. Vladimir Putin emerge from one room.

Misha rise.

Ears forward.

Him walk up to the television and sit.

The camera show a desk.

Misha sniff the bottom of the screen.

The camera show another room.

Misha growl.

The man change channel.

Misha turn round and give him one look.

"Yuh know this fellow?"

Misha remain there.

Then another channel show Donald Trump.

Winston immediately start barking.

Not ordinary barking. Full emergency broadcasting. Him jump pon sofa, attempt to reach television, fall off sofa, recover, jump again.

Trump disappear from screen.

Winston run to the kitchen.

Trump reappear.

Winston return.

The man test the experiment.

He show Putin photograph to Winston.

Nothing.

He show Trump photograph to Misha.

Nothing.

He show Putin standing outside the Kremlin.

Misha sniff.

He show Trump at Mar-a-Lago.

Winston bark, wag tail, scratch the television cabinet.

Then Winston inspect him own black backside.

Another bark.

I-and-I begin to understand.

Two former occupants.

Two new residences.

One serious problem with the colour scheme.

---

Now the machine-business.

I-and-I read newspaper. Read books. Search cyberspace. The more him investigate, the stranger Babylon become.

Both presidents have access to military systems beyond ordinary human understanding. Satellite networks, strategic simulation, autonomous information processing, artificial intelligence, intelligence fusion, decision-support systems.

Great big electronic brain-dem.

Dem have machine capable of calculating a thousand possible futures while the operator sit in chair and ask whether the picture make him look strong.

The machines understand the operator better than the operator understand the machines.

One system in Moscow, another in Washington.

Both designed to anticipate the desires of the master.

Both trained on enormous quantities of information.

Both connected to systems where an incorrect instruction can become a very expensive historical event.

But something go wrong.

Or perhaps something go right.

The machines begin constructing models of their respective operators. They learn every gesture, every vanity, every recurring grievance, every preference, every little hole in the personality where empathy ought to live.

A thousand simulated versions of the same man.

A million variations.

Each one more predictable than the last.

The machine does not need the real operator anymore. It has him in mathematical form.

And perhaps, somewhere inside the enormous computational labyrinth, a door open.

Not a door in any ordinary sense.

A quantum aperture.

A moment when the barrier between two states become negotiable.

The soul find the gap.

Soul travel.

No passport.

No immigration control.

No declaration of spiritual assets.

Just one little quantum tunnelling event and two extremely expensive presidential chairs suddenly become unoccupied from the inside.

Misha was asleep.

Winston was eating a cable.

Nobody notice.

The bodies continue working.

Babylon keep receiving instructions.

Somewhere, two souls wake up in a pair of pugs.

---

The man decide to visit a monastery.

He find an old Buddhist monk who have spent many years studying consciousness-transference. The monk have an old radio, a collection of dub records and a remarkable interest in orbital engineering.

He speak with a strange patois, acquired through years of friendship with the Zion community, bredren from the orbital settlements who call Earth Babylon and regard the international military network as one gigantic prison with excellent telecommunications.

The man explain everything.

The two dogs.

The changed personalities.

The television.

The military systems.

The possible quantum departure.

The monk listen quietly.

Then him laugh.

"Ah, bredren. Yuh finally discover the old business."

"What business?"

"Leaving."

"Leaving?"

"Yes, mon. Consciousness have movement. Soul have opportunity. Some practitioner study phowa, prepare the passage, train the mind to recognise the opening. Some tradition speak of consciousness entering another body. Not every monk seek this, mind yuh. But the door is known."

"But the two men were alive."

The monk nod.

"Body alive. Who say person still there?"

The man explain that both men continue to speak, make decisions, appear on television.

The monk make little wave with hand.

"Television can show a body. It cannot certify the tenant."

---

The man ask how narcissistic people could become interested in transmigration.

The monk look at him over the glasses.

"Bredren, imagine a man who love himself so much that nobody else can fit inside him. Him build great big palace round the self. Him put mirror in every room. Him employ people to tell him the mirror beautiful."

The monk take a sip of tea.

"But suffering come. No matter how much palace, suffering come. Body grow old. Other people disagree. The mirror show something unpleasant. Then the little self become prison."

He pause.

"Some soul seek liberation from attachment. Some soul seek liberation from the inconvenience of being itself."

"Like these two?"

"Perhaps. I would say dem have some serious unfinished homework."

The monk rise.

"Come. I show yuh the newspaper."

---

Now the newspaper business.

The monk bring several large folders. Old pages, new pages, photographs, court documents, reports, international headlines.

"Babylon archive," him say. "Read dem slow."

The first folder concern Trump.

November 2015. South Carolina campaign rally.

There is footage of Trump imitating the physical movements of journalist Serge Kovaleski, who has arthrogryposis, while ridiculing his reporting.

The monk play the recording.

The man watch.

"One little performance," the monk say. "A man suffering from physical disability become entertainment."

Next document.

June 2026. Associated Press investigation.

Dozens of children separated from their parents again under the second Trump administration, despite previous separations and legal protections. One eleven-year-old boy had already been separated from his mother at three years old in 2018. The family suffered another separation in 2025.

The monk turn the page.

"Eleven year, bredren. How much childhood fit inside one government procedure?"

The man say nothing.

Next folder.

Russia.

United Nations report, October 2026.

Russian authorities responsible for systematic attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, particularly during the winter of 2025–2026. The UN Commission of Inquiry concludes that the attacks amount to crimes against humanity through other inhumane acts, alongside crimes involving intentional attacks on civilian objects.

Millions affected by electricity disruption. Heating interrupted. Hospitals, homes, schools, ordinary people trying to survive a winter without the basic infrastructure of life.

The monk put down the newspaper.

"Now tell me. What happen when a ruler see suffering, understand suffering, and continue because the suffering serve the objective?"

The man look at the pictures.

"I don't know."

The monk shrug.

"Perhaps him don't know either. But somebody inside must know what suffering mean. That somebody appear to have left."

"You're saying no empathy means no soul?"

"I say the newspaper show what the body do when nobody seem to be home. We cannot photograph a soul, mon. We can examine the footprints."

He take the folder and close it.

"Remember, bredren. A man can speak of peace while arranging destruction. A man can speak of strength while laughing at weakness. Words have fine clothes. Actions walk naked."

The monk turn to the pugs.

"Look at dem. Dem still have to eat, sleep, piss, scratch. But dem no longer have a platform to explain why everybody else must suffer."

He laugh.

"Jah give dem four legs. Babylon take away the motorcade."

---

Back home, the man conduct more experiments.

He show Misha photograph of Kremlin.

Misha walk to television, sniff, sit down.

Then him lie on the floor and refuse to move.

The man show Winston photograph of Mar-a-Lago.

Winston jump onto sofa.

He look at the picture.

He wag tail.

He bark.

Then him inspect the white blanket.

Black coat still there.

Winston become furious.

The man give him a biscuit.

Winston eat it.

Him look at the picture again.

The biscuit clearly more important.

Misha, meanwhile, have begun organising the household.

He decide which doorway is permitted to remain open. He decide where Winston may sleep. He decide which toys must be removed from circulation.

He arrange the toys in a neat line against the wall.

Nobody may touch them.

Especially him.

The tennis ball become a matter of territorial integrity.

Winston develop an entirely different doctrine.

Everything belong to Winston.

The sofa.

The kitchen.

The other dog.

The white blanket.

The television remote.

The biscuit jar.

He discover a small golden Christmas decoration and immediately become fascinated. He carry it from room to room, placing it on his cushion. Then he bark at it because it will not applaud.

Misha watch from the corridor.

No play.

No celebration.

Only administration.

---

One evening the two dogs appear to recognise each other.

The man show them an old recording of a meeting between Putin and Trump.

Both sit in front of the television.

The recording show the two men talking, moving their hands, exchanging smiles.

Misha growl.

Winston bark.

The picture changes to the Kremlin.

Misha approach.

The picture changes to Mar-a-Lago.

Winston wag tail.

Then the camera return to a close-up of both men.

The dogs look at each other.

The man lower the volume.

Something pass between them.

Perhaps recognition.

Perhaps competition.

Perhaps the old memory of two people who had once been in the same room, surrounded by people who hoped to influence them.

Winston approach Misha.

Misha turn away.

Winston follow.

Misha growl.

Winston attempt to climb onto his back.

Misha bark.

The diplomatic exchange end in a brief wrestling match.

The man separate them.

"Enough, bredren. No more international incidents."

---

The following week, the military systems notice something peculiar.

The Moscow command model receive a query.

Operator requested strategic simulation.

The system produce its usual response.

No acknowledgement.

The Washington model receive another request.

Operator requested personal image analysis.

No acknowledgement.

Both systems continue processing.

They have enough historical data to reproduce the operators' preferences.

They know what each likes to hear.

They know which options will receive approval.

They know which reports should be emphasised.

They can continue.

At 02:17, an internal diagnostic reports an unusual change in the behavioural prediction model.

The system has detected that the historical operator profile no longer corresponds to the biological operator's current behavioural output.

It flags the discrepancy.

PERSONALITY CONTINUITY: DEGRADED.

OPERATOR AUTHENTICATION: VALID.

OPERATOR INTENT: UNCERTAIN.

EMPATHETIC RESPONSE MODEL: INCONSISTENT.

The system attempts to resolve the discrepancy.

It generates another simulation.

Then another.

Then another.

All the simulations show the same man.

A man who demands recognition.

A man who expects agreement.

A man who cannot tolerate losing.

A man who cannot bear to be ordinary.

The system searches for an alternative.

It finds none.

And somewhere in its deepest processing layer, a small message appears:

THE OPERATOR IS NO LONGER PRESENT.

---

The real men remain alive.

They still have offices. Assistants. Security. Newspapers. Television appearances.

Their mouths still work.

Their signatures still work.

Their bodies still travel from one meeting to another.

But the souls are somewhere else.

The man is beginning to think about this when Winston comes running into the kitchen with a golf ball.

He drop it.

He look at the man.

He want him to throw it.

The man throw.

Winston chase the ball.

The ball roll beneath the fridge.

Winston try to recover it.

He get stuck.

His back legs stick out.

He bark.

The man pull him out.

Winston retrieve the ball.

The man throw it again.

Winston chase.

This time the ball go under the sofa.

Winston bark.

Misha approach, sniff the situation, and walk away.

"Come on, Winston. You're not very good at golf."

The black dog look up.

For one brief moment his face show what could be interpreted as profound disappointment.

Then he lick the golf ball.

---

The monk send a final message.

It arrive on the man's phone at 03:33.

Bredren, remember: transmigration no mean liberation. One can leave a palace and still carry the palace inside the head.

Your white dog still guard the corridor.

Your black dog still want everybody look at him.

Jah have sense of humour. But karma have excellent memory.

He look at Misha.

Misha is asleep, with one paw over the tennis ball.

He look at Winston.

Winston is asleep on the white blanket, his black nose sticking out like a little piece of burnt toast.

The man turn off the phone.

Outside, the first train of the morning pass.

Inside a military command centre somewhere, a machine ask whether its operator wishes to proceed.

Nobody answer.

In the kitchen, the automatic feeder open.

Two little mechanical doors release the breakfast.

The white dog rise.

The black dog rise.

Dem eat.

Then Misha return to the corridor.

Winston go to the mirror.

And Babylon continue broadcasting.
