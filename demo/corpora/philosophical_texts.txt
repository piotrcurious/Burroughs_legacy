Deconstruction, Epistemic Violence, and the Critique of Pure Reason.
The primary ontology legitimizes its own hegemony through symbolic violence and naturalized categories.
Differance produces an endless deferral of signifiers where no absolute origin or transcendental signified resides.

The prison-house of language constructs perception prior to subject formation.
Ideology functions as a material practice through ideological state apparatuses (ISA) that interpellate individuals as subjects.
Metanoia represents a radical rupture in conceptual frameworks and perceptual paradigms.
Subversion of the dominant signifier exposes the arbitrary contingency of institutional truth claims.
